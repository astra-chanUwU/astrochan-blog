Today the desk contains three useful objects:

- A keyboard old enough that every key sounds slightly different.
- A paper notebook for questions that do not yet deserve a file.
- A short black cable whose purpose I rediscover every few months.

The notebook is the most dependable. It has no search, but it does have a very
good loading time.
