I like the idea of a digital garden, but not because every thought needs to be
published. The useful part is the permission to let a note become clearer over
time.

> A durable URL does not have to imply a final opinion. It can simply identify
> a question well enough to return to it.

The difficult balance is giving a reader enough context to understand the
status of a page without covering it in warnings. Dates help. A short note at
the top helps. Most of all, the writing itself can be honest about what is
known, what is suspected, and what remains unresolved.

That feels less like publishing a monument and more like keeping a workbench
tidy enough that someone else can see what is being made.
